# Brig — authored deck and interior plan

One continuous 24-gun main battery, separate berth deck, framed hold and a furnished cabin on the lower after deck. The low preserved quarterdeck does not falsely promise a full standing room above the main gun deck. No Snow/trysail pole.

The existing hull offsets, dimensions, mast positions and stock-derived cannon total are preserved. Interior arrangements are original estimates informed by the historical analogues below; they are not recovered POTC retail rooms.

## Historical analogues

- [Royal Museums Greenwich: Gannet (1800), ZAZ4365](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-84156): Separate main battery, lower berth/storage deck and hold, accessed by physical companion openings; compact after accommodation. Sixteen-gun historical analogue, not the 24-gun game Brig; exact gun positions, dimensions and internal partitions are authored.
- [Royal Museums Greenwich: deck plan of Cruizer (1797)](https://prints.rmg.co.uk/products/the-deck-plan-of-the-cruizer-1797-j4868): Late two-masted brig arrangement vocabulary and a clear working deck. No museum plan image is copied; later brig analogue is not evidence of POTC retail geometry or a Snow mast.

## Quality and scope

The accepted detailed Bryg Mewa reference supplies the minimum structural quality bar: visible inner framing and ceiling, a real hollow hold, furnished cabin, true hatch and door openings and useful stairs. Geometry is built as closed timber solids with physical-scale wood grain UVs. Two palettes share the same geometry and natural materials.

The asset remains static. Engine sailing, buoyancy, firing logic, crew AI and a player controller are not supplied. Current numeric checks and imported-GLB review images are authoritative for the built result.
