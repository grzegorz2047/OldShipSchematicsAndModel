# Sources

This is the list of every source cited in the chapters and the appendix, each listed once. Each chapter numbers its
own sources [1], [2] …; the line "Cited in" gives the chapter and the number the source has there, for example
"05 [1]" is source 1 of [chapter 05](05-masts-and-spars.md); "A" is the
[appendix on handling the ship](appendix-sail-handling.md). The notes after each entry repeat what the chapters say
about access and licence. The confidence labels (period, secondary, our inference, reference model) are explained in
[chapter 01](01-ship-type-and-era.md).

Facts from these sources are summarised in our own words; pictures, plans and models from them are linked, not
copied.
