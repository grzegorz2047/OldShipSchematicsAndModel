# Glossary

This glossary collects the terms from the section "Terms used in this chapter" of every chapter and of the
appendix, in alphabetical order of the English term. The column "Chapter" says where the term is explained; "A" is
the [appendix on handling the ship](appendix-sail-handling.md). Several terms share a row when a chapter explains
them together.

Some English words have two meanings in this guide, for example *truck* (the disc on top of a mast, and a wheel of a
gun carriage) or *tack* (a rope at the clew of a course, and the manoeuvre). Each meaning has its own row. Where two
chapters give a term a different Polish name or a different explanation, both rows are kept, each with its chapter.
"(uncertain)" or "[uncertain]" means that we are not sure the Polish term was used in this sense. The confidence labels and the
sources are explained in the chapters; all sources are listed in [Sources](sources.md).
